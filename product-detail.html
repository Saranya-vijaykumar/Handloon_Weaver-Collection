The Legacy of Varanasi Zari Weaving

Rooted in the sacred ghats of Varanasi, this saree represents an unbroken lineage of handloom mastery. Pure mulberry silk yarn, hand-reeled and dyed with natural mineral extracts, meets electroplated silver-gilded metallic threads known as zari.

Every motif is hand-punched onto wooden jacquard cards before being translated by the weaver's hands onto the traditional handloom, resulting in a fabric of substantial weight, fluid drape, and timeless luminescence.

Preserving Your Heirloom

  • check_circle Dry Clean Only: Always entrust to an eco-friendly specialist experienced in handling pure silk and metallic zari threads.
  • check_circle Muslin Storage: Store wrapped in unbleached cotton or muslin cloth in a dry, dark wardrobe away from direct sunlight.
  • check_circle Airing: Air the saree outdoors in indirect shade every few months to maintain natural fiber vitality.

Master Weaver Pandit Ramkishan

Pandit Ramkishan has spent over four decades seated at the sunken pit loom in his ancestral home in Varanasi. Taught by his father at age twelve, he preserves rare brocade techniques that take upwards of 120 hours per masterpiece.

"When the shuttle passes through the silk warp, it carries not just thread, but 600 years of songs and silence."
