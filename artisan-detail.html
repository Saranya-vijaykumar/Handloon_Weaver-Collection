Master Weaver Pandit Ramkishan

Varanasi Handloom Cluster • 4th Generation Pit Loom Weaver

"When the shuttle passes through the silk warp, it carries not just thread, but 600 years of songs and silence."

Pandit Ramkishan began weaving at age 12 beside his grandfather in the sacred lanes of Madanpura, Varanasi. He specializes in the endangered 'Kadwa' technique where each gold motif is woven individually without loose floats on the reverse.

Through the Handloom Weavers Collective, his entire artisan cooperative receives direct living wages with zero middlemen exploitation. Every piece produced under his watchful eye represents hundreds of hours of unhurried manual mastery, certified mulberry silk, and 24-karat gold electroplated silver zari.
