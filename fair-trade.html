The Ethical Standard

Four Pillars of Fair Weaving

Our unbreakable commitment to the artisans who bring our heritage textiles to life.

01

Living Wages

Artisans set their own piece rates. We guarantee wages 40% above market standard, adjusted continuously for regional inflation.

verified Direct Pay Model
02

Ancestral Autonomy

We preserve traditional motifs and heritage techniques without industrial compromise, ensuring creative sovereignty remains in the village.

history_edu Heritage First
03

Interest-Free Advances

Protecting weavers from predatory rural moneylenders, we provide 50% advance funding for raw materials before a single thread is spun.

handshake Zero Debt Trap
04

Community Welfare

10% of all collective profits are reinvested directly into weaver healthcare funds, solar loom upgrades, and localized artisan schools.

groups Collective Growth

Traditional retail markup chains siphon wealth away from the maker. We flip the paradigm. By cutting out multi-tier middlemen, over 65% of our final retail value flows directly back to the artisan and their community fund.
