Ancestral Heritage

The Timeless Legacy of Varanasi

For over two millennia, the ghats of Varanasi have been synonymous with divine textiles. Mentioned in Buddhist scriptures and Mughal chronicles, Varanasi silk is celebrated for its ethereal lightness combined with opulent metallic brocades that catch light like temple lamps.

Care & Maintenance

Heirloom textiles require tender preservation to last across generations.

dry_cleaning

Dry Clean Only

Always entrust your Varanasi brocades to professional eco-friendly dry cleaners experienced with pure metallic zari.

air

Breathable Storage

Store wrapped in unbleached muslin cotton in a dry, dark wardrobe. Avoid plastic covers which trap humidity.

wb_sunny

Gentle Airing

Air out the fabric in shaded, indirect breeze every few months to maintain the natural luster of silk proteins.
